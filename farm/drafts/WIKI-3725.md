### 2. New section after the Job Waiting description

````markdown
#### Printer idle: waiting for filament

Sent when a printer stands still because its waiting jobs cannot start for want of filament. It has its own checkbox per provider (**on by default**).

A message is sent when a printer has **been idle for 5 minutes**, at least one job is held back for filament on it, and nothing else could be started there. It says which printer, which filaments to load (a short spool with its slot and grams, e.g. `🔴 PLA Lava Red (A3 has 196 g, needs 505 g)`) and which jobs wait.

- **Once per standstill.** Again only after the printer did something else in between (it printed) or the filaments to load changed.
- **Only with Loaded filament first.** Without it a job does not wait for filament, so there is nothing to announce.
- **Switched-off printers.** A printer that auto-off (its smart plug) switched off counts as idle, but only when the plug's last state is OFF, the last known AMS trays exist, no plate clear is pending and no waiting job would switch the printer on again. A printer that is only disconnected (plug still on), one without a plug and one whose trays are unknown do not count. The last known trays are kept in memory; after a server restart an off printer counts once it has been on again.
- **"Any model" jobs.** You get one message per idle printer that lacks filament for the job, each saying what to load there.
- **Not for** a printer waiting for a plate confirmation, held by a sensor interlock or drying, printing, paused, offline, or with an empty plate changer. Those have their own events.
- **Restart:** the state is saved, so a restart neither starts the 5 minutes over nor repeats a message.

!!! note "Short spools"
    A job waiting for a spool that holds too little is announced at once with Job Waiting, once per job and spool. A filament that is only missing keeps waiting for its priority as before.
````

### 3. Variables

```markdown
**Printer Idle: Waiting for Filament:**

- `{printer}` - Printer name
- `{job_count}` - Number of waiting jobs
- `{jobs_wait}` - `1 job waits` or `2 jobs wait`
- `{jobs}` - The jobs, joined with ` · `
- `{load}` - One line per filament to load
- `{filament}`, `{filament_type}`, `{color_name}`, `{color_hex}`, `{color_emoji}`, `{slot}` - As for Job Waiting
```
