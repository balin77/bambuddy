## 1. `docs/features/print-queue.md`

### 1a. New section "Prepare the Next Jobs"

Insert as a new `###` section **after** `### Where to Put the Filament` (from the #3634 wiki draft) and before `### Actions and Permissions`:

````markdown
### Prepare the Next Jobs

A printer that is busy also shows what its **next jobs** need ([#3640](https://github.com/maziggy/bambuddy/issues/3640)). Under the entries of the printer (or alone, when it has none) quiet gray rows say:

```
Next: Bracket needs PETG Black, PLA Blue
Can be replaced: A3 (PLA Yellow), A4 (PLA Green)
Then: Lid needs PLA White
Free: B2
```

You can load these filaments **while the printer prints**, so it does not stand idle once the print has ended.

- **Which jobs.** The next one or two jobs of the printer that miss a filament, in the order the queue takes them: jobs queued for this printer, and **Any &lt;model&gt;** jobs that only this printer can take. A model job that several printers could take is not listed, because it is not known which printer gets it. A job scheduled for later is listed when it is due before the running print ends.
- **Which slots.** The lines are worded as in [Where to Put the Filament](#where-to-put-the-filament). A slot is never offered when the running print uses it, when the printer would switch to it if a running spool ran out (AMS filament backup: the same filament and colour on the same nozzle), or when a nearer job needs it. The second job is offered what the first would not take.
- **No rows.** A printer that does not report what its running print uses, for example a print started from the slicer, shows no rows. The list never guesses.

!!! note "Not a to do"
    The rows are a suggestion. They are **not counted** in the badge, send **no notification**, and nothing is reserved on the printer. The queue starts jobs as before. The rows follow the printer and the queue with the next scheduler pass, a few seconds after a change.
````

## 2. `docs/reference/api.md`

### 2a. Field table

Add this row to the field table of `### Get To Do List` (after the `groups[].entries` rows):

```markdown
| `groups[].prepare[]` | Printer groups only: what to prepare for the next jobs of the printer, not counted in `count`. Empty when there is nothing to prepare. Each item has `job` (`{id, name}`), `position` (`1` for the next job that misses a filament, `2` for the one after; numbered again for the jobs you may see), `filaments` (as in `entries[].filaments`) and `slot_hint` (as in `entries[].slot_hint`, or `null` while the printer reports no trays) |
```

Then add after the table:

````markdown
`prepare` is advice computed by the scheduler. It reserves no slot and changes nothing. The field is additive: clients that ignore it see the same response as before.
````
