````markdown
### Inventory Colours for a Specific Printer

The inventory colours also work when the job goes to **one printer** or to **several printers** instead of "any printer of a model" ([#3649](https://github.com/maziggy/bambuddy/issues/3649)). Use it when the printer is fixed (it has the right plate or nozzle) and the colour is on the shelf, not loaded yet. It needs **Show inventory colours** to be on (see [Show Inventory Colours](#show-inventory-colours)) and the job to go to **Queue** or **Schedule**.

#### One Printer

Select one printer. Under **Filament mapping** the dropdown of each filament has a second group below the loaded slots:

| Group | What it lists |
|-------|---------------|
| *(loaded slots)* | The slots loaded on this printer, as before |
| **From inventory (not loaded)** | The types and colors in your [inventory](inventory.md) that are not loaded on this printer, with the same stock hint and warnings as in the filament override |

A filament that picked an inventory color:

- reads **Waits for filament** instead of a ready mark, and still shows the color the file asks for
- gets no slot; the other filaments keep theirs
- is left out of the cost estimate, which says it is partial
- keeps its left/right badge on a dual-nozzle printer, and the hint under it says into which nozzle's AMS the color goes

A color from the inventory is always a **forced** color: the job waits for exactly that color on the printer, so the slot has no **Force color match** box. Choose a loaded slot again to give the pick up.

#### Several Printers

With several printers selected there are no slot dropdowns, so the dialog shows the **Filament Override** list reduced to the inventory group. A pick makes **every selected printer** wait for that color on itself. The colors you leave alone stay automatic, as before. The picks are kept when you switch between one printer, several printers and "any printer of a model".

#### How the Job Starts

The job waits on the printer until the color is loaded. How it then starts is the queue's usual behavior, and the hint under the slot says which one applies:

| Setting | What you see | What to do |
|---------|--------------|------------|
| **Loaded filament first** on | *Waiting for filament: …* | Load the color. The job starts by itself |
| **Loaded filament first** off (default) | The job is held | Load the color, then press **Start** |

The [To Do List](#to-do-list) shows such a job as **Load filament** like any other filament wait.

#### Print Now

A print that starts at once can only use what is loaded. Under **ASAP** the inventory group is not shown, and **ASAP** is disabled while a color from the inventory is picked, with the reason: *A colour from the inventory is not loaded yet. Queue or schedule the job, or pick a loaded slot.*

#### Stock Hint for a Waiting Job

If the color is in the inventory and none is left, a job that waits for it on a specific printer says so in its waiting reason, for example *Waiting for filament: PLA (Lava Red) – none left in inventory*, and the **Filament Out of Stock** notification is sent once (see [Stock Hint](#stock-hint)). Add a spool to the inventory and load it, and the job continues.

#### Editing

Editing a queued job that waits for an inventory color shows the pick again. Saving keeps it.

!!! note
    With [Spoolman](spoolman.md) the inventory group is not offered, as for the filament override.
````
