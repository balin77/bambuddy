## Description

The To do list says in words where a filament can go ("Replace: A3 (PLA Lava Red, 196 g left)"). At the printer, with a spool in hand, I want to see the slots and which one the spool goes into, and to assign the spool without leaving the list. This PR does that for **Load filament** entries and for the Prepare rows. No migration, no new setting, no new dependency.

**The picture.** A load entry draws the printer's AMS: one panel per unit (`AMS-A`, `AMS-B`, each AMS-HT, the external holder), every slot with its colour, name and grams. The filaments to load are drawn in a row **inside the unit panel**, each exactly above the slot it goes into, joined by an arrow, so arrows never cross and a card is as wide as its slot. Slot states, with text and symbol as well as colour: goes out (replaced), empty, fits already, locked (the running print uses it), unknown. A filament that has no slot is named under the picture.

- Where a filament goes is a **reading of the scheduler's own decision** (`SlotHint`, #3634): a short spool goes to its own tray, a missing filament to a free slot on the right nozzle and then to a replaceable one, a slot of the running print is never taken. This PR adds no new decision; the backend only serves the slots the scheduler already looked at (`all_slots`, `needed`, `running` of its pass).
- Units nothing goes into are folded behind a "Show AMS-B, C" toggle. Dual-nozzle printers label the nozzle. An AMS 3H (9 slots) wraps into rows of at most 5 columns, the incoming row above each row.
- An "Any &lt;model&gt;" job shows the picture of the printer likely to take it first (idle and connected, fewest spools to take out), with a switch to the other candidates.
- A Prepare row (#3640) gets a compact picture.
- An entry shows its picture once; the old slot sentence is the fallback when a printer reports no slots.

**Assign in the entry.** "Assign spool for A3" opens the matching inventory spools inside the entry (no dialog): enough grams first, then by grams, a spool that is too short dimmed with the reason, each with weight, state and storage location. One tap assigns it to that slot. The body is the former `AssignSpoolModal` content extracted into a shared `AssignSpoolPicker` (queries, filter, mismatch confirmation, Spoolman branch, scan, toasts); the modal uses the same body and behaves as before, its tests are unchanged. Also **Scan label** (the existing QR flow, into the same slot), **Add new spool** (the spool form) and **Set up slot** (the configure dialog, for a slot without RFID). After an assignment the list refreshes. When the slot still holds the old spool, the entry says "Put the new spool in first, then assign it."

Permissions: Assign and Scan `inventory:update`, Add new spool `inventory:create`, Set up slot `printers:control`. Spoolman: assigning goes through the existing Spoolman branch; with Spoolman down there is no assign button (as on the printer card, #3430); "Add new spool" is not offered.

## Changes Made

**Backend**
- `queue_todo.py`: `place_filaments` (where each filament goes, from the hint), `picture_dict` (units, slots, states, incoming), `build_todo` adds `ams_picture` (load entry) / `ams_pictures` (model entry) and a compact picture on prepare rows. Additive fields of `GET /queue/todo`.
- `print_scheduler.py`: `SlotHint` keeps the per-pass snapshot (`all_slots`, `needed`, `running`); nothing about a decision changes.
- `api/routes/print_queue.py`: grams and looks of the spool assigned to a slot (built-in inventory, one query), spools in stock per incoming filament, idle printers. Spoolman mode: only the grams the pass already has, no count, no live call per slot.
- `schemas/print_queue.py`: the picture schema.

**Frontend**
- `QueueTodoAmsPicture.tsx`: `AmsPicture`, `AmsPictures` (one CSS grid per unit, `gridColumn` = slot index).
- `AssignSpoolPicker.tsx` (extracted), `AssignSpoolModal.tsx` (uses it), `QueueTodoSlotActions.tsx` (Assign, Scan, Add new spool, Set up slot).
- `QueueTodoList.tsx`, `QueueTodoLauncher.tsx` (Escape does not close the panel while a dialog of an entry is open), `api/client.ts`, i18n in all 16 locales.

## Additional Notes

**Deviations from the mockups (honest list)**
- The incoming row sits inside the unit panel, not above it.
- Unit labels read "AMS-A", "AMS-B" (the app's own labels).
- An AMS 3H wraps at 5 columns, with the incoming row above each row.
- Grams come from the inventory spool assigned to the slot; without one, the printer's own percentage is shown.
- Spoolman: no grams or stock count unless the pass already has them, no "Add new spool".
- The picture lags the live trays by up to one scheduler pass.
- "Put the new spool in first, then assign it" is an added note, not in the mockups.
- There is no separate "job does not fit" entry (the proposal had one). Edit job and Move are offered on Load filament entries instead (step 2); Move only for jobs pinned to a printer.
