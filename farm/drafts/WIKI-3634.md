### 1b. New section "Where to Put the Filament"

Insert as a new `###` section **after** `#### Filament for a Printer Model` (and before `### Actions and Permissions`):

````markdown
### Where to Put the Filament

A **Load filament** entry also says where the filament can go ([#3634](https://github.com/maziggy/bambuddy/issues/3634)). The line under the filament is one of:

| Line | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| **Free: A2** | The printer reports an empty slot. All empty slots are listed |
| **Can be replaced: A4 (PLA Green)** | Every slot is loaded. These are the slots no waiting job needs, with the filament in them. Take out one of them |
| **Every loaded filament is needed by a waiting job** | Nothing can be taken out without a waiting job missing it. You have to choose |
| **Replace: A2 (PLA Lava Red, 12 g left)** | A loaded spool has too little filament for the job. Only that spool is named |

Slots are named as on the printer card: **A4** is AMS A slot 4, **HT-A** an AMS-HT, **Ext** the external spool holder and **3H-7** a slot of an AMS 3H. The colour names are the ones of the printer card.

- **Several lines.** A dual-nozzle printer gets a line per nozzle (**Left nozzle**, **Right nozzle**), and a filament for one nozzle is only offered slots of that nozzle. An **Any &lt;model&gt;** entry gets one line per printer, for example **X1C-2: Can be replaced: A4 (PLA Green)**. Three printers are shown, the rest are behind **+N more**.
- **Unknown filaments.** When a waiting job's file cannot be read, the entry adds **1 waiting job has unknown filaments** (or the number). That job may need a slot, so check before you take a spool out.
- **No hint.** A printer that has not reported its trays (switched off, just connected) shows no line.

**How a slot counts as needed.** The queue uses the same filament matching as the scheduler. A slot is needed when any waiting job that could go to this printer would use it: jobs queued for this printer, jobs queued for its model that fit it (model, location and the creator's printer scope), and staged, scheduled and later jobs as well as the waiting one. The slots the running print uses are never offered. The external spool holder is offered only on a printer without AMS slots.

**A spool that is too short.** When a job waits although its filament is loaded, because the spool holds less than the job prints, the entry says **Not enough PLA Lava Red: A2 has 12 g, the job needs 40 g**. Spools that AMS Filament Backup counts together are named together (**A2 + A3 have 25 g**). The line under it reads **Replace: A2 (PLA Lava Red, 12 g left)** and names only the short spool. A spool of the wanted filament is never offered as free or replaceable.

!!! note "A hint, not a reservation"
    Nothing is reserved and nothing is changed on the printer. The queue starts jobs as before. The line follows the printer and the queue with the next scheduler pass, a few seconds after a change. A spool of the same material but another colour can count as needed, because the scheduler would use it for a job.

!!! info "What you see depends on your access"
    The line names no job. The count of unknown jobs covers only the jobs you may see in the queue, and printers outside your printer scope are not listed.
````

### 2b. Field table

Add these rows to the field table of `### Get To Do List`, after the `entries[].printers` row:

```markdown
| `entries[].slot_hint` | `load_filament` of a printer only: where the filament can go, or `null` while the printer reports no trays. Absent or `null` for other kinds |
| `entries[].slot_hint.state` | `free` (an empty slot is reported), `replaceable` (every slot is loaded; `slots` are the ones no waiting job needs), `all_needed` (`slots` is empty) or `replace` (a spool is too short; `slots` are the short spools, each with `remaining_grams`) |
| `entries[].filaments[].short`, `short_trays[]`, `required_grams` | `short` is true when the filament is loaded but in too little amount; `short_trays[]` are `{ams_id, tray_id, remaining_grams}` of the counted spools and `required_grams` is what the job prints. `false`, `[]` and `null` otherwise |
| `entries[].slot_hint.slots[]` | `ams_id`, `tray_id`, `is_ht`, `is_external`, `ams_type`, `nozzle` (`left` / `right` on a dual-nozzle printer without a Filament Track Switch, else `null`), and for a loaded slot `type`, `color`, `tray_info_idx`, `tray_sub_brands` (empty for a free slot) |
| `entries[].slot_hint.unknown_jobs` | Waiting jobs you can see whose filaments could not be read |
| `entries[].slot_hint.by_nozzle[]` | Dual-nozzle printer without a Filament Track Switch: one `{nozzle, state, slots}` per nozzle of the entry. Empty otherwise |
| `entries[].slot_hints` | `load_filament` in a model group only: the same object plus `printer_id`, for each printer of `printers` that has a hint and that you may see. Empty otherwise |
```

Then add after the table, before "Groups are ordered by the queue position of their first job.":

````markdown
The hint is advice computed by the scheduler. It names no job, reserves no slot and changes nothing. The fields are additive: clients that ignore them see the same response as before.
````
